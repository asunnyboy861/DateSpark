Subscription FAQ

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find and tap DateSpark
  5. Choose "Cancel Subscription" or change your plan

Or visit: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings > Restore Purchases. This will sync your subscription status with your Apple ID.

What's the difference between Monthly and Yearly?

Both plans offer the same features. The Yearly plan offers a 58% discount compared to paying monthly.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can continue using premium features until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll revert to the free version with up to 10 favorites.

I purchased Lifetime but it's not showing

Go to Settings > "Restore Purchases" to restore your Lifetime access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my favorites saved?

Yes! Your favorites are stored locally on your device. Free users can save up to 10 favorites; Premium users have unlimited favorites.

Does DateSpark work offline?

Yes! All 200+ curated topics are available offline. Only the AI-powered custom topic generation requires an internet connection.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All your data is stored locally on your device. We don't collect or transmit any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
